# Blind review: expiry publishes a lone submission

**Status:** APPROVED through PD-002

Owner and contributor submit four-dimension reviews hidden from both sides until either both submit or the review window expires. Both reviews publish immediately on the second submission. If only one side submits by expiry, that review publishes alone, labelled that the counterpart did not submit. Ratings of 1 or 5 require a written rationale.

## Consequences

- Prevents a specific manipulation: a party can't suppress negative feedback about themselves by simply not submitting their own review.
- Non-submitters get no review-completion incentive — there's no reward for waiting out the window.
- Published reviews are immutable except through audited admin invalidation.
